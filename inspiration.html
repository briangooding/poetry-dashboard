Each dot is one of your tags or collections, placed by how original and how inventive the poems under it are, and colored by how freely their lines run. Scores are percentiles against your own body of work, so 50 means average for you. Pick a dot to see its poems, then open any poem to read it.

Originality (across)How rare the vocabulary is within your corpus. High means words you seldom reach for.
Creativity (up)Lexical range in the first 60 words. High means few repeats and constant new material.
Fluidity (color)How often a line break falls mid-phrase, such as a line ending on “of” or “into”, or the next line picking up with “and” or “through”. Teal flows; rust breaks where the phrase ends.
